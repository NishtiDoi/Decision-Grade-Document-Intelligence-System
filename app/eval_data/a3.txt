DEED OF RENT

This Deed of Rent is made at Bangalore, this 1st day of March 2025, effective
from 1st March 2025 between Mr. Ramesh Kumar,
S/o Late S. Kumar, aged 52 years, residing at
No. 12, 3rd Cross, Indiranagar, Bangalore – 560038,
hereinafter called the HOUSE OWNER of the ONE PART

AND

Mr. Ankit Sharma,
S/o P. Sharma, aged 28 years, residing at
Flat No. 301, Sai Residency, Whitefield, Bangalore – 560066,
hereinafter called the TENANT of the OTHER PART.

The terms House Owner and Tenant wherever they may occur shall, unless repugnant
to the context, mean and include their respective heirs, successors and assigns.

WHEREAS the House Owner is the sole and absolute owner of the residential premises
situated at Flat No. 301, Sai Residency, Whitefield, Bangalore – 560066.

WHEREAS the Tenant has applied to the House Owner for grant of tenancy of the said
premises for residential living purposes.

NOW THIS DEED OF TENANCY WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. That the tenancy shall be initially for a period of 11 months with effect from
1st March 2025 and ending on 31st January 2026 and may be renewed thereafter
with mutual consent of both parties.

2. That the rent payable by the Tenant to the House Owner shall be Rs. 18,000/-
(Rupees Eighteen Thousand only) per month, payable on or before the 5th day
of every succeeding month, in addition to maintenance charges payable to
Sai Residency Apartment Association.

3. That the Tenant has paid a sum of Rs. 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand only)
as interest-free refundable security deposit, the receipt of which is hereby
acknowledged by the House Owner. The said amount shall be returned at the time
of vacating the premises after adjusting rent dues, electricity charges,
water charges, maintenance charges and cost of damages, if any.

4. That the premises has a separate electricity meter and the Tenant shall pay
electricity charges as per meter readings directly to the Electricity Board.

5. That property tax, water tax and sewerage tax shall be paid by the House Owner.
Water consumption charges based on usage shall be paid by the Tenant.

6. That the fittings and fixtures are in good condition and shall be returned in
good condition, normal wear and tear excepted. Cost of damages, if any, shall
be borne by the Tenant.

7. That the Tenant agrees to a minimum stay of six months. The Tenant shall give
two months’ prior notice before vacating the premises. In case of shorter notice,
the Tenant shall pay two months’ rent as compensation. The House Owner shall
also give two months’ notice to terminate the tenancy.

8. That the Tenant shall not sublet or assign the premises or any part thereof.
The premises shall be used strictly for residential purposes only.

9. That the Tenant shall keep the premises clean and hygienic and shall not make
any structural alterations without prior written consent of the House Owner.

10. That the House Owner or his authorized representative shall have the right
to inspect the premises at reasonable times with prior intimation.

11. That the Tenant shall not keep any pet animals in the premises.

12. That the Tenant shall hand over all keys, fittings, electrical fixtures and
the electricity board card to the House Owner at the time of vacating the premises.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, both the parties have signed this Deed on the date mentioned above.
