RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This lease begins on March 1, 2025 and ends on February 28, 2026.

RENT: Tenant shall pay $2,000 on the 1st of each month. Late payments after the 5th will incur a $75 late fee.

MAINTENANCE: Landlord will maintain all major systems (heating, plumbing, electrical). Tenant must report any issues within 24 hours.

TERMINATION: Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice. Early termination by Tenant will result in forfeiture of security deposit.

PETS: No pets allowed without written consent. Unauthorized pets will result in immediate lease termination and a $500 cleaning fee.
